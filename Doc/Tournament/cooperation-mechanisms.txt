INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
These are observed decisions, not assertions that a bot must belong to a predetermined class.
The assertions check successful execution and legal selected actions, not preferred victims.
Same decisions on a finite set of boards do not establish universal absence of recognition.
A soft ratio penalty can leave the strongest attack unchanged; close-choice probes are included.
Raw differences can also include unused pass endpoints; read FT before interpreting a target.
Borg can override friend status for a one-army target with >5 attacking armies; easy-copy uses 2.
Hunter prey is held fixed across identity arms: prey preference can outweigh its copy penalty.
Slave master is held fixed too: its master exclusion is independent of program recognition.
The neutral-target board removes local prey/master weighting, without clearing target state.
In endgame master/prey=3 has no land: Slave can mark dead[51]=1; Hunter can stop hunting.
prey-master-copy isolates protecting/targeting player 2 from recognizing its filename.

Frank/Frank+ contain context- and buffer-gated recognition; these isolated calls do not
reproduce complete Assignment/turn histories, all continents, toggles, or every cooperation mode.
