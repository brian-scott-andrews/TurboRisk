Some TRPs select a particular player to pursue, while others recognize specific
bot filenames and adjust their strategy.

Hunter
Chooses a "prey" player and favors attacks against that player.

Grudge
Targets the opponent responsible for capturing the most of its former
territories; humans win ties.

Kingmaker
Targets the strongest opponent according to its strength score.

Piranha
Specifically favors attacking human players.

Australian09, Descartes09, Endeavour09
Have filename-aware rules involving Frank, Zotob, Australian and the other 09
variants. These affect threat assessment and attack planning, not necessarily
unconditional targeting.

Frank / Frank+
Recognize Australian-family opponents and change certain behavior. Frank+ also
contains Vexer-specific checks.

Australian+
Recognizes Vexer and adjusts finishing thresholds and risk calculations.
That is caution around Vexer, not simply "attack Vexer first."

Renaming a bot can change how opponents treat it, even when its code is
identical. That is why the renamed cooperation-OFF controls measure
identity-dependent behavior, not a perfectly isolated cooperation switch.
