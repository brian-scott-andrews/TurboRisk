Playing a game

Each player's turn consists of three steps, in this order:
  1. Getting and placing new armies.
  2. Attacking (optional)
  3. Fortifying (optional)

Getting and placing new armies

At the beginning of your turn TurboRisk calculates how many new armies you have to reinforce your territories. Depending on which cards you own and on the Cards handling option in the Options > Players menu, a dialog can appear which shows your cards and give you the possibility to trade them for armies (see Rules).

To place your armies simply left click on one of your territories. The ALT, SHIFT and CTRL keys can speed up the process: if you keep one of them pressed while clicking you place 5 armies, with two of them you place 10 armies and with all three you place 25 armies.

Attacking

Left click on one of your territories (where you attack from), and then on one of its neighboring country (which you attack). A dialog appears that shows the territories involved and gives you three possibilities:
  •  Attack  button: performs one attack (throw dice).

  •  Do or die button  button: automatically repeats attacks until you conquer the enemy territory or your are left with 1 army.

  •  Retreat  button: suspends the attack and closes the dialog.

If you checked the box Advanced options in Attack window in the Preferences, you are offered two more attack options:

  •  Attack until my armies ≤ N  button: automatically repeats attacks until you conquer the enemy territory or until the last attack leaves you with N armies or less, where N is any number of your choice.

  •  Attack until enemy's armies ≤ N  button: automatically repeats attacks until the last attack leaves the enemy with N armies or less, where N is any number of your choice.

In the dialog window there is a check box which allows the automatic transfer of all of your attacking armies in the conquered territory (if conquered...). Unless this check box is active, if your attack is successful the troop move dialog appears. In this dialog you can decide how many armies move in the conquered territory, and then press the  OK  button.

Two special buttons can speed up your game:  All  moves all your armies in the conquered territory, while  Balance  divide them equally between the two (both of them close the dialog window).

You can perform as many attacks as you want before ending your turn.

Fortifying your position

You can fortify just once in a turn. Depending on a specific parameter in the Options > Rules menu this movement may or may not end your turn.

Left click on one of your territories (where you move from), and then on one of its neighboring country (where you move to). The troops move dialog appears, which is the same you got after a successful attack.

Ending your turn

If fortification is allowed only at the end of the turn and you fortify, your turn automatically ends. Otherwise, you can always explicitly terminate your turn by clicking  End turn  toolbar button or by pressing the space bar.
