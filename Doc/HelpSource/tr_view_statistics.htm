Statistics

The Statistics window gives you a summary of what's happening in the game: for each player, you can see
  • how many territories he occupies
  • which continents he controls (if any)
  • how many armies he owns
  • how many cards he holds in hand
  • how many armies he will get at the next trade in of cards (only if Cards Trade In is based on progression)

This window can be configured: right click on it and select which one information you want to see in the table.

The order of the players in the window corresponds to their turn sequence.
