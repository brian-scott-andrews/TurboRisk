Frequently Asked Questions

Why aren't Secret missions supported by TurboRisk?

Because Secret missions make the goal of the game much less clear, and that would very difficult to manage in TRPs. They would require a much more sophisticated coding, and their strategy will very likely would be much less effective.

Why can't I create maps with number of territories different by 42, or different links between them?

Most of the TRPs build their strategy on the structure of the classical Risk map. The making of an AI player able to play effectively on any kind of map would require a huge effort, and at the same time it would likely result in a weaker opponent.
