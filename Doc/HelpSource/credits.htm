Credits

Turbo risk has been written by Mario Ferrari.

Many people supported TurboRisk and contributed to make it a better program, either reporting bugs or sending comments, ideas, suggestions, custom maps and TRPs. The author thanks them all with gratitude.

Check the Help > About menu item for an updated list of the major contributors.

TurboRisk scripting is based on the Delphi-style, free RemObjects Pascal Script runtime interpreter.
