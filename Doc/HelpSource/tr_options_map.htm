Choosing a Map

Through the Options > Map menu item you can choose the map to use during a TurboRisk game.

Different maps have different sizes, graphic styles, fonts. It's important to emphasize, however, that all of them have 42 territories grouped in 6 continents, and that the relationships between the territories and the continents are the same in all of the maps.
For example, Alberta borders with Alaska, Northwest Territory, Ontario and Western US: in any map these links are the same.

Notwithstanding this limitation, which is a conscious design choice, changing the map may improve your esperience of the game, simply because some maps better fits the screen size of the your video, or simply because you like that particular map more than other maps.

The Map window shows a list of the available maps, with the file name followed by a short description.
When you selecting a map in the list, TurboRisk shows a small preview of the map itself and some additional information about its size, author and revison note.

Mark the checkbox on the left of the map name in the list to make it the current map.

Maps can be changed at anytime, even during a game.

TurboRisk release includes many different maps. Many of them have been written by TurboRisk users. The section Making a custom map describes how anybody can create his/her own TurboRisk map.
